# 📊 Employee Attrition Prediction and Analysis

An end-to-end Machine Learning project that analyzes employee attrition patterns, predicts employee turnover, and provides actionable HR recommendations through data-driven insights and visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations because replacing experienced employees is both costly and time-consuming. This project explores the factors influencing employee turnover using data analysis and predictive modeling.

The project combines Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Machine Learning, and business insights to help Human Resource (HR) teams identify employees who are at a higher risk of leaving and develop effective retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Identify key factors contributing to employee turnover.
- Build predictive models for employee attrition.
- Compare multiple Machine Learning algorithms.
- Generate actionable business recommendations for HR.
- Visualize important trends using informative charts.

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis identified several factors associated with higher employee attrition:

- Employees working frequent overtime showed a higher likelihood of leaving.
- Employees with lower monthly income experienced higher attrition.
- Certain departments and job roles showed comparatively higher turnover.
- Limited career growth and fewer promotions were associated with increased employee exits.
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance played an important role in retention.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Reduce excessive overtime and promote a healthy work-life balance.
- Strengthen employee engagement initiatives.
- Improve career development and internal promotion opportunities.
- Review compensation strategies for high-risk employee groups.
- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys.
- Implement targeted retention strategies for departments experiencing higher turnover.
